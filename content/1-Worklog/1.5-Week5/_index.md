### Week 5 Objectives:

* During the week of 26/10/2026 - 01/11/2026, completed 14 CloudJourney labs on networking, content delivery, application firewalls and EC2 (000003, 000092, 000111, 000019, 000020, 000074, 000010, 000094, 000130, 000026, 000097, 000004, 000032, 000045), and also watched 3 videos on VPC, real-world architecture and the Solutions Architect Associate exam.
* Able to independently plan IP ranges for a multi-AZ VPC: calculate the usable IPs of each subnet (after subtracting the 5 IPs reserved by AWS) and avoid overlapping with networks that will be connected later.
* Able to build a VPC with public and private subnets, an Internet Gateway, a NAT Gateway and 2 route tables; able to explain why packets follow the route with the longest prefix.
* Able to distinguish Gateway Endpoint from Interface Endpoint, Security Group from NACL, VPC Peering from Transit Gateway, and know when to use which.

* Understand the CPU credit mechanism of the EC2 T family and how to collect memory metrics to right-size instances.
