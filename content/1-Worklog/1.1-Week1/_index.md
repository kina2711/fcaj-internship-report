### Week 1 Achievements:

* Understood the new AWS Free Tier programme (from 15/7/2025):

  + Receive $100 credit on sign-up
  + Receive another $100 after completing 5 tasks in the AWS Console
* Able to distinguish the Free Plan from the Paid Plan:

  + The Free Plan charges nothing for 6 months and closes the account automatically when the credit runs out or expires
  + The Paid Plan can use all services, but charges once you exceed the credit
* Know how to check credit at Billing → Credits; credit applies only to a list of services, and services outside that list are charged directly to the card.
* Lessons learned when creating an account:

  + If the information is already tied to an old account, the new account does not count as a new customer, so it does not receive the full $200 credit
  + If you close the account, you must ask AWS Support to reopen it
* No usable AWS account for practice yet; I plan to borrow a Visa card to register a new account and have it open during week 2.
